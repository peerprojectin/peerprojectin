The Peer Project // Mentee Application

If you're preparing for boards or an entrance exam, it helps a lot to have someone who's been through it recently. That's more or less what we do here - pair you with a senior who has, over text on our Discord server, for roughly 8 to 12 weeks, that is, ~3 months. It's free.

What you're getting yourself into

It's not tutoring. Your mentor won't teach you chapters or do your homework. What they will do is help you figure out how to study, where to find good material, and how to plan your weeks around it.

It does need effort from your side, though. Check in when you said you would, and be honest about what you did and didn't get done, even if it's not much. And if something comes up, tell your mentor instead of just going quiet.

Everything stays on Discord. Only you, your mentor and TPP admins can see your channel, so there's no need to move to WhatsApp or Instagram, or to share your number or real name.

And we can't promise everyone a mentor. It depends on how many we have for your field. If we can't match you, we'll tell you by email rather than leave you waiting. If you haven't yet, read how it works and the mentee guide first.

How we handle your data

We only ask for what we need to match you. Not your full name, and not your phone number, address or school. Your application is read by TPP admins and no one else, and your mentor only sees what's relevant to mentoring you.

Your email is for our decision, nothing else. If you're under 18, a parent or guardian needs to know you're applying and be okay with it. The rest is on our privacy page.
