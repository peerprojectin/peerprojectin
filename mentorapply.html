The Peer Project // Mentor Application

Assuming you've recently been through boards or an entrance exam, you probably still remember what you wish someone had told you back then. That's more or less what you'd be doing here - guiding a student who's preparing for the same thing right now, over text on our Discord server, for roughly 8 to 12 weeks, that is, ~3 months.

What you're getting yourself into

It's not tutoring. You won't be teaching chapters or solving homework, and we'd rather you didn't. What you will be doing is helping your mentee plan their weeks and find the right material. And don't just go inactive or ghost them if there's a change of plans.

It does take time, though not a lot of it. Everything is text-based, so you reply when you're free, but you do need to keep replying. Your own exams will get in the way at some point, and that's fine, just tell us early. Ghosting your mentee hurts them more than any bad advice would.

You're also not a counsellor, and nobody expects you to be. If a mentee brings up something heavy, point them to our support page and let a TPP admin know.

And not everyone gets in. After this form there's a short written questionnaire, and we pick mentors based on that and this form. If you haven't yet, read how it works and the mentor guide first.

How we handle your data

We only ask for what we need to check that you're eligible and to match you well. No phone number, no address. Your application is read by TPP admins and no one else, and your mentee only sees your display name and whatever is relevant to mentoring them.

Your proof of eligibility is probably the most sensitive thing we ask for. Only TPP admins look at it, and only to check that you're eligible. We delete it as soon as we've made a decision on your application, and if that takes longer than 30 days for whatever reason, it gets deleted automatically anyway. There's more on this next to the upload field.

Your email gets your questionnaire link and our decision, nothing else. The rest is on our privacy page.

Proof of eligibility

A scorecard / marksheet / school report-card / student-ID / college-ID / anything works. Only TPP admins can see it, and only to verify your eligibility. It isn't copied, shared, or used for anything else, and nobody opens it except to check that you're eligible.

Your document is deleted as soon as we make a decision on your application. You'll know because you'll get an approval or rejection email from us.

If we haven't made a decision within 30 days of you uploading it, for example because the admins are unavailable or something goes wrong on our end, it's deleted automatically anyway. That deletion runs on a schedule inside our database and doesn't need any of the admins to do anything. The schedule is set up once, and we don't change or pause it.

Before uploading, blur or cover your address, phone number, roll or application number, and photo. Don't upload Aadhaar or any other government ID.
