The Peer Project // Privacy

This page covers what happens to the information you give us when you apply to The Peer Project, as a mentee or a mentor. We've tried to keep it short and specific. If something here isn't clear, just ask us.

What we collect

From mentees: the name you want to be called, your age, class, board and subjects, the fields you want help in and what specifically you're stuck on, your exam year, how often you can check in, the languages you're comfortable in, your Discord username and your email. Your Reddit username, where you heard about us and anything else you tell us are optional.

From mentors: the same, mostly, plus what you're doing right now, the classes and fields you can mentor, any results you share, how many mentees you can take on, optional profile links, your proof of eligibility, and your answers to the questionnaire.

We never ask for your full name, phone number, address or school, and we'd rather you didn't put them anywhere in the form either.

What we use it for

Only for running the programme: deciding who gets in, matching mentees with mentors, setting up your channel on Discord, and emailing you about your application. We don't sell it, share it for marketing, or use it for anything unrelated. No newsletters either.

Who sees it

Your application is read by TPP admins and no one else. If you're matched, your mentor or mentee sees your display name and whatever is relevant to mentoring, nothing more. On the Discord server, everyone can see your display name, which is why we ask for a pseudonym or preferred name instead of your real one.

Proof of eligibility

This is probably the most sensitive thing we ask mentors for, so it gets handled separately. Only TPP admins can open it, and only to check that you're eligible. It isn't copied, shared, or used for anything else, and every time an admin opens one, it's logged with who opened it and when.

It's deleted as soon as we make a decision on your application, and you'll know because you'll get an approval or rejection email from us. If we haven't decided within 30 days of you uploading it, it's deleted automatically anyway. That runs on a schedule inside our database every day and doesn't need any of the admins to do anything. The schedule is set up once, and we don't change or pause it.

Where it's stored

Applications and proofs are stored with Supabase, in their Singapore data centre, in a database that only our own backend can read. Emails to you are sent through Resend. The site itself runs behind Cloudflare, and the forms use Cloudflare's bot check to keep spam out, which means Cloudflare sees basic technical details like your IP address.

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to count page visits. It doesn't set cookies or follow you around other sites, and we don't use any other analytics or tracking. The questionnaire does save your answers in your browser's localStorage while you write them, so a closed tab doesn't lose them. They stay on your device until you submit, and we clear them after.

Retention

We keep applications, questionnaire answers and matches until the cohort ends, and they're deleted automatically 3 months after that, the same way proofs are. Proofs of eligibility go much sooner, as above.

If you're under 18

A parent or guardian needs to know you're applying. You confirm that on the form, along with agreeing to this page.

What you can ask us

You can ask for a copy of what we hold about you, ask us to fix something, or ask us to delete your application, at any point. Email summons@peerproject.in from the address you applied with, and we'll take care of it. If you withdraw before decisions go out, we simply won't consider your application.

If this changes

If we change how we handle your data, we'll update this page and the date below.
